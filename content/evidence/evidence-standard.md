# Evidence Standard

Evidence may support **or challenge** a hypothesis, decision, outcome, benefit, pattern or insight.

## Strength

| Strength | Typically means |
| --- | --- |
| Weak | anecdote, single observation, unvalidated self-report |
| Moderate | small sample, one context, consistent direction |
| Strong | multiple sources or contexts, pre-registered measure, operational data |

Weak evidence must never be presented so that it looks like strong evidence.

## Good looks like

- source, date, method and owner recorded
- disconfirming evidence recorded with the same care as supporting evidence
- every claim in a decision traceable to a record

## Anti-patterns

- "stakeholders told us" as a strength-neutral statement
- evidence created after the decision it supports
