# Mission Health

Mission health has nine dimensions: outcome clarity, evidence strength, team confidence, stakeholder alignment, decision velocity, delivery flow, risk visibility, value confidence and operational readiness.

## What this means for us

Health is never reduced to a single percentage or a traffic light. Leadership should always be able to see **why** a mission looks healthy or unhealthy, and which dimension is weakest.

## Anti-patterns

- RAG status chosen to manage a conversation
- health assessed by people with no contact with the mission
