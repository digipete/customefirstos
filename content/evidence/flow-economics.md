# Flow Economics

Flow economics measures the speed and cost of learning.

## Why we do this

Two missions can spend the same money and produce very different amounts of certainty.

## What this means for us

We measure time and cost from mission start to first experiment, meaningful evidence, significant decision, delivered outcome and realised value, plus decision latency and experiment cycle time.

## Good looks like

- cost to first experiment tracked per mission
- stage durations visible without asking the team
- slow points attributed to constraints, not to people

## Anti-patterns

- measuring throughput of activity rather than learning
- using flow measures to compare individuals
