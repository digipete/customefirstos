# Continuous Architecture

```architecture-diagram continuous-architecture

```

Architecture does not finish when an experiment is approved.

As evidence changes, the architecture can change.

As an intervention scales, architectural concerns change.

As risks become real or are disproven, controls can change.

## Frame

Make the decision and outcome explicit before selecting technology.

## Shape

Apply the principles and domains to expose important choices and trade-offs.

## Identify uncertainty

Find what must be learned before the team can make the next decision responsibly.

## Test & Learn Experiment

Generate evidence through a bounded intervention with appropriate guardrails and stop conditions.

## Learn and decide

Use what actually happened to scale, test again, modify or stop. Testing again or modifying loops learning back into the process. Scaling increases exposure and therefore may increase the assurance required.
