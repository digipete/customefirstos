# Architecture principles

These are not rules for architects. They are decision-making tools for teams.

1. **Start with the outcome** — understand the problem before choosing the technology.
2. **Make the smallest safe decision** — decide only what we need to decide now.
3. **Design for change** — keep decisions, components and technologies replaceable where possible.
4. **Use what exists — until it stops helping** — respect the partner environment without becoming trapped by it.
5. **Preserve future choice** — prefer openness, portability, interoperability and explicit trade-offs.
6. **Treat data as part of the service** — understand ownership, movement, quality, semantics and value from the start.
7. **Build trust into the design** — design in security, privacy, accessibility, resilience and operability.
8. **Prove rather than predict** — use evidence, experiments and observable behaviour rather than architectural opinion.

```guidance principle
Don't make a bigger decision than the evidence allows.
```

Use the principles together and proportionately. They support judgement; they do not replace it.
