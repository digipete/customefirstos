# Architecture at CustomerFirst

Architecture creates confidence to make the next decision.

Architecture helps CustomerFirst teams understand uncertainty, make proportionate technology decisions and safely learn what should happen next.

**Not a gate. Not a phase. Not a document.**

```architecture-diagram four-part-model
```

Principles tell us how to think.

Domains ensure we consider the whole system.

Evidence tells us what we actually know.

Assurance determines whether we have enough confidence to act.

These ideas continuously inform one another. They are not a waterfall.

## Architecture in delivery

```architecture-diagram evidence-led-delivery
```

```guidance assurance
Architecture helps us make the experiment safe enough to run, and the resulting decision strong enough to act on.
```

We are not architecting a complete solution and then testing whether it works.

Architecture helps shape the mechanism through which we learn what should happen next.

## A confidence-building system

Architecture at CustomerFirst is a confidence-building system.

Architecture is not about proving that a design is correct. It is about creating enough confidence to make the next good decision.

It works inside multidisciplinary teams from the start. It connects the problem, the decision, the assumptions, the experiment, the evidence and the next move.

The outcome is not simply a better design. CustomerFirst should leave partner organisations more capable of owning, operating and changing the service than when we arrived.
