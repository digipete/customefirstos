# Treat data as part of the service

Understand data ownership, movement, quality, semantics and value from the start.

## Why this matters

A service cannot be understood separately from the information it creates, uses and shares.

## What this means in practice

Trace the data journey with the service journey. Name owners and meanings. Minimise data and test quality assumptions.

## Questions to ask

- What information is genuinely needed?
- Who owns and can explain it?
- Where does it move and why?

## During a Test & Learn Experiment

Use representative data where appropriate and safe. Measure quality, access, meaning and operational effort.

## Watch out for

- treating data integration as plumbing
- copying data without ownership or purpose
