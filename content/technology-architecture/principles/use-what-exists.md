# Use what exists — until it stops helping

Respect the partner environment without becoming trapped by it.

## Why this matters

Existing environments contain capability, investment, constraints and operational knowledge. Ignoring them can prevent sustainable ownership; accepting every constraint can prevent learning.

## What this means in practice

Start with existing services and standards. Test whether a constraint is real. Make departures deliberate, transparent, proportionate and de-risked.

## Questions to ask

- What existing capability can we reuse?
- What evidence shows this constraint matters?
- Who will own an alternative after CustomerFirst leaves?

## During a Test & Learn Experiment

Use the partner environment where it supports the learning. If it prevents the experiment, test the constraint or use a bounded alternative with an ownership path.

## Watch out for

- bypassing governance quietly
- treating inherited technology as either sacred or worthless
