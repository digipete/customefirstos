# Preserve future choice

Prefer openness, portability, interoperability and explicit trade-offs.

## Why this matters

Lock-in can make later evidence unaffordable to act upon. Future choice is valuable when uncertainty remains.

## What this means in practice

Prefer open formats and documented interfaces. Understand exit costs. Record deliberate dependencies and review triggers.

## Questions to ask

- What choice does this close?
- Can data and knowledge move elsewhere?
- What would exit cost?

## During a Test & Learn Experiment

Test portability or replacement where future choice materially affects the decision.

## Watch out for

- paying for theoretical optionality
- ignoring supplier and proprietary dependencies
