# Prove rather than predict

Use evidence, experiments and observable behaviour rather than architectural opinion.

## Why this matters

Architecture claims often sound certain before they meet real systems, users and operating conditions.

## What this means in practice

Write assumptions as hypotheses. Choose a credible test. Record observations and confidence, including inconvenient evidence.

## Questions to ask

- What are we assuming?
- What could we observe?
- What evidence would change our mind?

## During a Test & Learn Experiment

Put the riskiest important assumptions in the experiment and connect results to a named decision.

## Watch out for

- experiments designed to confirm a preference
- documents presented as evidence

## Related guidance

Use this principle with the CustomerFirst Decision Framework, Test and Learn guidance and evidence standard.
