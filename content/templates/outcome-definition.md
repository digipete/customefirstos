# Outcome Definition template

Copy this template into your mission folder, or use it directly in GitHub. It is designed to be usable outside the application.

## Fields

- **Outcome statement (customer-facing)**
- **Baseline measure and source**
- **Target direction and rationale**
- **Measurement method**
- **Counter-measures (what must not get worse)**
- **Review points**

## Notes

Keep the completed outcome definition in Markdown so it stays readable, reviewable and portable.
