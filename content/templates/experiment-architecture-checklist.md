# Experiment Architecture Checklist

Use only the prompts that matter to the decision and exposure. This is not an approval form.

- What decision are we trying to inform?
- What technical assumptions matter?
- What data is involved?
- What external systems are involved?
- What is the exposure?
- What could cause harm?
- What guardrails are required?
- What evidence are we collecting?
- What would make us stop?
- What happens if the experiment succeeds?

Record answers in the existing Test & Learn Experiment material rather than producing a separate architecture pack.
