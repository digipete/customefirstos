# Mission Canvas template

Copy this template into your mission folder, or use it directly in GitHub. It is designed to be usable outside the application.

## Fields

- **Mission name**
- **Partner organisation**
- **Customer need**
- **Problem statement**
- **Intended outcomes and measures**
- **Users affected**
- **Primary hypotheses**
- **Known constraints**
- **Investment envelope**
- **How we will know to stop**

## Notes

Keep the completed mission canvas in Markdown so it stays readable, reviewable and portable.
