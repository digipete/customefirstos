# Experiment template

Copy this template into your mission folder, or use it directly in GitHub. It is designed to be usable outside the application.

## Fields

- **Mission and hypothesis**
- **What we will do**
- **Method**
- **Participants**
- **Success criteria (written before starting)**
- **Cost estimate**
- **Start and end date**
- **Evidence to be captured**
- **Decision this experiment will inform**

## Notes

Keep the completed experiment in Markdown so it stays readable, reviewable and portable.
