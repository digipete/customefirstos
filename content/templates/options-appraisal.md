# Options Appraisal template

Use this as a thinking aid, not a scoring exercise that disguises judgement.

## Decision

What decision are we trying to make, and by when?

## Evidence and constraints

What do we know? What remains assumed? Which constraints have been tested?

## Options

Include **Do nothing** and every genuinely credible alternative.

For each option consider:

- outcomes
- cost
- risk
- speed
- reversibility
- sustainability
- user impact
- operational impact

## Trade-offs

What benefits, costs and constraints would we accept with each option?

## Recommendation and review trigger

State the recommendation, evidence, confidence and what would cause the decision to be reviewed.
