# Decision Record template

Copy this template into your mission folder, or use it directly in GitHub. It is designed to be usable outside the application.

## Fields

- **Question**
- **Context**
- **Mission and domain**
- **Evidence considered (with links)**
- **Options considered**
- **Trade-offs accepted**
- **Decision**
- **Owner and decision date**
- **Review date**
- **Outcome at review**

## Notes

Keep the completed decision record in Markdown so it stays readable, reviewable and portable.
