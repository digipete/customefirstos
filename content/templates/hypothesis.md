# Hypothesis template

Copy this template into your mission folder, or use it directly in GitHub. It is designed to be usable outside the application.

## Fields

- **Statement: We believe that ... for ... because ...**
- **Underlying assumption**
- **Importance (low/medium/high)**
- **Uncertainty (low/medium/high)**
- **Evidence required to move confidence**
- **Current confidence**
- **Owner**
- **Linked experiments**

## Notes

Keep the completed hypothesis in Markdown so it stays readable, reviewable and portable.
