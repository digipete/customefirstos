# Mission Review template

Copy this template into your mission folder, or use it directly in GitHub. It is designed to be usable outside the application.

## Fields

- **Outcome and current understanding**
- **Lifecycle position**
- **Hypothesis status summary**
- **Evidence added since last review**
- **Economics: investment and value by state**
- **Health by dimension**
- **Decisions needed now**

## Notes

Keep the completed mission review in Markdown so it stays readable, reviewable and portable.
