# Architecture Decision template

Copy this template into your mission folder, or use it directly in GitHub. It is designed to be usable outside the application.

## Fields

- **Title and status**
- **Context**
- **Decision drivers**
- **Options considered**
- **Decision**
- **Consequences (positive and negative)**
- **Reversibility and review date**

## Notes

Keep the completed architecture decision in Markdown so it stays readable, reviewable and portable.
