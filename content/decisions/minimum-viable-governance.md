# Minimum Viable Governance

Governance should increase decision quality and speed at the same time.

## Why we do this

Governance that only adds assurance latency transfers risk from the decision to the delivery.

## What this means for us

We govern by domain (strategy, commercial, delivery, architecture, technology, data, AI, people, governance, investment), at the smallest forum that holds the evidence.

## Good looks like

- assurance attends the mission rather than the mission attending assurance
- decision latency measured and reviewed
- risk owned by a person, not a board

## Anti-patterns

- approval chains longer than the work
- boards that receive information but take no decisions
