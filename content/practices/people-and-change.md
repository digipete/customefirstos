# People and Change

Making adoption part of the design, not an afterthought.

## Why we do this

People and Change shapes decisions that are expensive to reverse later, so the practice is held to a shared standard across every mission.

## What this means for us

People and Change works inside the squad from the start of a mission, contributes hypotheses and evidence, and updates this guidance when missions show it is wrong.

## Good looks like

- involved from Discovery, not invited at Deliver
- opinions expressed as testable hypotheses
- standards applied consistently across missions
- learning written back to the pattern library

## Anti-patterns

- People and Change as a review gate at the end
- standards that exist only in one person's head
