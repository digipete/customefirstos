# Beliefs

Our operating model rests on a small number of beliefs. They are assumptions, so they are testable.

## Why we do this

Naming beliefs makes them challengeable. A belief that no mission ever contradicts is probably useful. A belief contradicted repeatedly should be revised in the open.

## What this means for us

We write beliefs down, tie guidance back to them, and revisit them when operational evidence conflicts.

## Good looks like

- uncertainty is reduced most cheaply early
- multidisciplinary teams make better trade-offs than sequential handovers
- most delivery constraints are organisational, not technical
- stopping work on good evidence protects value

## Anti-patterns

- beliefs used to close down debate
- beliefs that cannot be contradicted by any observation
