# Why CustomerFirst exists

Public services are rarely failing because people lack effort. They fail because the system around them makes good work expensive.

## Why we do this

Transformation is usually organised around programmes and outputs. Programmes protect plans. Missions protect outcomes. CustomerFirst exists to change services in ways that hold up under evidence, not under presentation.

## What this means for us

We organise multidisciplinary teams around customer outcomes, reduce uncertainty deliberately, and treat every mission as a source of learning for the next one.

## Good looks like

- a named customer outcome behind every piece of work
- evidence available before large commitments
- learning that survives the end of a mission
- the operating model improving because of real delivery

## Anti-patterns

- transformation measured by activity volume
- business cases that never get revisited
- learning that leaves with the people who left
