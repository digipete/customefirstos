# Principles

Principles exist to help people make good decisions without asking permission.

## The principles

1. **Start with the customer need**, expressed in their words, not ours.
2. **Make the uncertainty explicit** before choosing an approach.
3. **Design the smallest useful experiment** that could change our mind.
4. **Decide with evidence, and record the decision** with its review date.
5. **Distinguish potential value from realised value**, always.
6. **Stopping is a valid successful outcome** when evidence disproves the proposition.
7. **Reuse before you invent**; check the pattern library first.
8. **Keep knowledge portable**: Markdown in Git, data in open structures.
9. **Make constraints visible** rather than routing around them quietly.
10. **Leave the operating system better** than you found it.

## Anti-patterns

- principles quoted to win arguments rather than to make decisions
- principles with no observable behaviour attached
