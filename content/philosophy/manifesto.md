# CustomerFirst Manifesto

We value:

- **Customer outcomes** over delivery milestones
- **Evidence** over confident opinion
- **Small deliberate experiments** over large committed plans
- **Decisions with owners** over consensus without accountability
- **Reusable learning** over one-off success
- **Portable knowledge** over tooling convenience

There is value in the items on the right. We weigh the items on the left more heavily when we must choose.

> This is evolving guidance. It is expected to change as missions produce evidence about what actually works here.
