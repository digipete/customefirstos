# Anti-patterns

- **Pilot theatre** — a pilot with no hypothesis and no decision attached
- **Benefits inflation** — modelled value reported as realised value
- **Discovery drift** — discovery with no cost ceiling and no exit criteria
- **Gate governance** — assurance applied as a queue rather than as a contribution
- **Solutioned outcomes** — outcomes written as the thing someone already wants to build
- **Silent constraints** — teams routing around organisational blockers without recording them
- **Percentage complete** — progress rising while confidence falls
