# Pattern Library

A pattern is a reusable response to a recurring situation, supported by evidence from real missions.

## Status

- **Candidate** — proposed, observed once
- **Emerging** — observed in two or three missions
- **Proven** — independently supported across four or more missions
- **Retired** — superseded or contradicted by later evidence

Patterns get stronger as independent missions accumulate evidence. "Observed in 1 mission" and "supported by evidence from 7 missions" must never look the same.

## Anti-patterns

- patterns promoted by seniority rather than evidence
- patterns with no context statement, applied everywhere
