# Adaptation

Adaptation is the point of measuring.

## Why we do this

Teams that cannot change direction convert learning into sunk cost.

## What this means for us

We review evidence against hypotheses at defined points and decide to continue, change, scale, pause or stop.

## Good looks like

- decisions recorded with the evidence that triggered them
- pivots framed as progress
- stopped work celebrated when justified

## Anti-patterns

- reviews that only ever conclude continue
- pivots hidden inside re-planning
