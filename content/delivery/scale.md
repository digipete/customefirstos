# Scaling

Scaling asks where else this holds true.

## Why we do this

Scaling before understanding the mechanism reliably reproduces the packaging, not the outcome.

## What this means for us

We identify the mechanism behind the result, test it in a second context, and record it as a pattern once independently supported.

## Good looks like

- mechanism understood, not just the artefact
- second-context evidence before wide roll-out
- pattern written to the library

## Anti-patterns

- mandating a solution across services on single-mission evidence
