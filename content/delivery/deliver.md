# Delivery

Delivery is where evidence becomes an operating service.

## Why we do this

Evidence that never reaches live service produces no value.

## What this means for us

We deliver thin slices into live use, with operational readiness treated as part of the work rather than a later phase.

## Good looks like

- real users on the real service early
- support, data and assurance involved from the start
- rollback and recovery understood

## Anti-patterns

- big-bang releases
- operational readiness discovered at go-live
