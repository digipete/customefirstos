# Discovery

Discovery is how we replace assumption with understanding.

## Why we do this

Most expensive delivery mistakes are made confidently, early, and without contact with users or frontline staff.

## What this means for us

We spend proportionate time understanding demand, failure demand, constraints and existing evidence before defining outcomes.

## Good looks like

- users and frontline staff spoken to directly
- existing data and prior missions reviewed first
- assumptions written as testable hypotheses

## Anti-patterns

- discovery that produces a report and no hypotheses
- open-ended discovery with no cost ceiling
