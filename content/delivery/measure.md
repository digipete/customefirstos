# Measurement

Measurement closes the gap between intent and effect.

## Why we do this

Without measurement, delivered output is indistinguishable from delivered value.

## What this means for us

We measure the named outcome, record the evidence and update value confidence honestly.

## Good looks like

- baseline captured before the change
- value states moved only when evidence supports it
- negative results published

## Anti-patterns

- success declared at go-live
- benefits claimed from modelled figures
