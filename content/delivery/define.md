# Define

Define is where a mission commits to what it is trying to change.

## Why we do this

Ambiguous outcomes cause every later argument in the mission.

## What this means for us

We write the outcome, the measures, the primary hypotheses and what evidence would change our minds.

## Good looks like

- a measurable outcome statement
- named primary hypotheses with importance and uncertainty
- explicit non-goals

## Anti-patterns

- outcomes written as solutions
- measures chosen because they are easy to collect
