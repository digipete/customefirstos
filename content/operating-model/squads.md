# Multidisciplinary Squads

A squad holds all the disciplines needed to move a mission forward without handovers.

## Why we do this

Sequential handovers between disciplines are the single most reliable way to slow transformation down and lose context.

## What this means for us

Squads are small, stable for the life of a mission, and include the professions the mission actually needs rather than a fixed template.

## Good looks like

- a squad can go from question to evidence without external dependency
- roles are named individuals, not resourcing categories
- the squad shares one set of hypotheses

## Anti-patterns

- part-time squads across five missions
- design finishing before engineering starts
