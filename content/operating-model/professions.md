# Professions

Professions hold the craft: standards, growth, hiring and quality.

## Why we do this

Missions optimise for outcomes now; professions optimise for capability over time. Both are needed.

## What this means for us

Each person has a mission (what they work on) and a profession (how they get better). Communities of practice run across missions.

## Good looks like

- shared quality bars applied consistently across missions
- practice guidance updated from real mission evidence
- time protected for community of practice

## Anti-patterns

- professions functioning as approval boards
- craft standards that exist only verbally
