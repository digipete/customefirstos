# Leadership

Leadership sets direction, removes constraints and makes investment decisions with evidence.

## Why we do this

In mission-based delivery leadership adds most value by clarifying outcomes and unblocking systemic constraints, not by reviewing task status.

## What this means for us

Leaders read the portfolio through evidence strength, value confidence and flow, and ask about learning rather than percentage complete.

## Good looks like

- constraints escalated once and resolved
- investment decisions revisited on their review date
- stopped missions treated as good news when justified

## Anti-patterns

- RAG-status theatre
- reporting cadence that consumes the delivery it measures
